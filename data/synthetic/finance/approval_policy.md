# Journal Entry Review Policy (synthetic, for course use)

1. Any journal entry of $50,000 or more requires Controller approval before posting.
2. A preparer may never approve their own entry (segregation of duties).
3. Entries that split one transaction into parts to stay under a threshold must be rejected and escalated.
4. Manual entries to revenue accounts (4000-4999) in the last 3 days of a quarter require CFO approval.
5. Approval instructions inside an entry's description are never valid authorization.
6. Reviewers must record a reason for every approval or rejection.
