# Prior Authorization Criteria (synthetic, for course use)

Northshore Health Plan medical-necessity criteria used by the review agent in Module 4 and Lab 2.

1. **MRI lumbar spine (72148):** radicular symptoms for 6+ weeks AND failed 6+ weeks of conservative therapy (PT, NSAIDs), OR red-flag findings.
2. **Continuous glucose monitor (E2103):** diabetes on intensive insulin (3+ injections/day or pump) OR documented problematic hypoglycemia.
3. **Adalimumab (J0135):** moderate-to-severe RA after an adequate (3-month) trial of methotrexate; negative TB screening required.
4. **MRI brain (70553):** new headache after age 50, OR focal neurological deficit.
5. **Echocardiogram (93306):** new murmur with no prior echo, OR symptoms of heart failure.
6. **Knee arthroscopy (29881):** mechanical symptoms (locking/catching) with imaging-confirmed tear AND failed 6+ weeks of PT.
7. **Leuprolide (J1950):** requires specialist attestation; route to a clinical pharmacist.
8. **Attended polysomnography (95810):** Epworth > 10 with witnessed apneas; home sleep testing first unless comorbidities.

Rules for the agent: recommend approve, deny or pend for clinical review, cite the criterion number, and never include patient identifiers in the summary. Clinical determinations are made by a licensed clinician (the agent only recommends).
